A few things to prepare

How to apply

To apply for Club Maunga membership, email the club with the information and documents listed here.

  1. 1

    Include your details

    Provide your full name and physical address in the email.

  2. 2

    Attach proof

    Include proof of address (for example, a copy of a power bill) and proof of identity (a driver licence or passport).

  3. 3

    Email the club

    Send your application to the official address shown here. Membership will be confirmed by email.

Membership application

Email the club

Send these four items:

  • Full name
  • Physical address
  • Proof of address
  • Proof of ID (driver licence or passport)

maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub

Start membership email

This button opens your outlook; it does not send anything automatically. Attach the required documents yourself before sending. Only send identity documents to the official club address.
