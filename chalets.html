Before you book

Good to know

01

Chalets are available to club members only.

02

All bookings must run Friday to Sunday.

03

All chalets are on the mountain, so you can ski to and from your stay.

Before planning your stay:

  • You must be a club member to use the chalets.
  • Bookings must run from Friday to Sunday.
  • Check the adult and child limits for your chosen chalet.
